=== GPU sizing (measured 2026-11-05) ===
Measured on the depth-4 run: 168 sequences in 267 min on 2x T4 = 3.18 GPU-min
per sequence. The T4's compute capability is 7.5, so BoltzGen's cuEquivariance
kernels switch themselves off ("Using kernels: False"); capability 8.0+ (4090
8.9, A100 8.0, L40S 8.9, H100 9.0) enables them. BoltzGen takes every visible
GPU (devices = torch.cuda.device_count()) and the sequences are independent, so
throughput scales with GPU count.

An epoch is 72 min; after generation and the 22-block submission margin, 66 min
are usable for scoring. Speed factors below are ESTIMATES from FLOPs plus the
kernels, not measurements — the first rented box must measure them.

config            factor  candidates/epoch   $/h   $/day
2x T4 (measured)      1x                41     -       -
2x RTX 4090           3x               124  0.68      16
4x RTX 4090           3x               248  1.36      33
2x L40S               4x               166  1.98      48
2x A100 80GB          4x               166  2.38      57
2x H100 SXM           8x               331  4.80     115

20 epochs/day. At the measured 48% win rate: 9.6 wins/day = $2,403/day, so the
GPU bill is 0.7% to 4.8% of revenue. Cost per sequence is therefore the wrong
thing to optimise.

What matters instead is reliability: one missed epoch costs $120 in expectation,
more than a day of the price difference between a cheap unverified host and a
reliable one.

And the open question is not throughput but whether candidates past the fourth
help at all. The measured curve (best-of-N against the top four real
submissions) is 14% at N=1, 33% at N=2, 33% at N=3, 48% at N=4 — still rising,
but our candidates share donors and lineage, so it must flatten somewhere
unmeasured. Buying 331 candidates per epoch may buy nothing over 40.
